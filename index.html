Matrix Dynamics (Figure 5.15 & 5.16)

The matrix splits at the Likert midpoint of 2.5 on both axes. Two distinct behavioral trajectories emerge from practitioner evaluations[cite: 1]:

Trend A (Inverse Relationship): High-frequency activities concentrate in lower impact zones, while severe activities remain confined to lower frequency zones[cite: 1].
Trend B (Positive Outliers): WA1 (Late Architectural Changes) sits isolated as high-impact and high-frequency[cite: 1]; WA19 (Unnecessary Travel) sits isolated as low-impact and low-frequency[cite: 1].
